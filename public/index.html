6 paragraphs → 1 line

ABC Packing List

headlamps: accounted for

Two lists, one Annapurna trek. A password-protected packing and shopping checklist with separate profiles, real prices in NPR, and a deeply satisfying progress bar. Built so we'd stop arguing about who owns the headlamp.

Simple VPN

billing while off: $0.00

A personal WireGuard VPN that costs roughly nothing because the server only exists while you need it. Start it from your phone, stop it when you're done, and let the whole disk disappear with it.

audioandroid

Footnotes

audio walks with opinions

A geofenced audio guide that starts talking when you reach a stop — 66 walks in 51 places across 18 countries, from Istanbul to Gyeongju. Four storytellers per walk, cream paper and loud ink, and none of “and on your left.” Works in a tunnel, on a plane, in 2019.

Encore

five legends, one voice: yours

Five legends, one stage, your face and voice. Hand Encore a photo and a 30-second audition and it paints you into the scene and performs the speech in your accent — Gettysburg included. History's greatest hits, now with better casting.

02 — the lore

jei·ti·nho

/ʒejˈtʃiɲu/ · noun, Portuguese

The art of getting something done with whatever you've got, usually with less planning than recommended and more creativity than strictly necessary.

100% homemade

This is the family edition. Malu has the taste. Bernardo has the terminal. Nothing here has a roadmap, a sprint, or a stakeholder.

If a project is on this page, it's because we wanted it to exist. Sometimes it replaces a spreadsheet. Sometimes it replaces an argument. Once it replaced a very expensive VPN subscription.

  1. 01

    No meetings.

    We're married. We talk enough.

  2. 02

    Ship it weird.

    Then polish it until it looks intentional.

  3. 03

    If it isn't fun, it doesn't ship.

    The only KPI we respect.
